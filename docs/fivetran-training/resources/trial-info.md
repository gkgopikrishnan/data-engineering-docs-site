# 📝 Trial Information

## Fivetran Free Trial

Fivetran offers a **14-day free trial** for new users to experience their fully automated data pipeline services. During this trial period, you can:

- Connect your data sources (e.g., databases, marketing tools, file-based sources) to your destination (e.g., Snowflake, BigQuery).
- Sync data automatically without writing any code.
- Monitor the performance and success of your syncs in real-time.
- Set up your transformation pipelines using **dbt** integration.

### dbt Cloud Free Trial

dbt also offers a **free trial** for dbt Cloud, providing access to its powerful data transformation features. With the free trial, you get:

- Access to **dbt Cloud** for managing and deploying transformations in the cloud.
- The ability to **schedule** and **automate** dbt runs directly within your data warehouse.
- Integration with data sources connected through **Fivetran**.

### Getting Started with Your Free Trial

1. **Sign Up for Fivetran**  
   Go to [Fivetran's Sign-Up Page](https://fivetran.com/signup) to start your free trial.
   
2. **Sign Up for dbt Cloud**  
   Sign up for a free trial of [dbt Cloud](https://getdbt.com/dbt-cloud/).

3. **Integrate Fivetran with dbt**  
   Once both trials are activated, follow the steps outlined in this guide to connect your data sources with **Fivetran** and set up **dbt Cloud** for transformations.
